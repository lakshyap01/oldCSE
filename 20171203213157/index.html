Our Expertise

Welcome to
Center Stage Events

We craft inspiring & trendy Events

Where creativity meets definition and expertise meets innovation. Established in 2007, Center Stage Events has conceptualised and managed over 500 events in multiple industry domains such as IT, Hospitality, Home Improvement, Home Automation, Commerce, Retail, Banking sector and more. Headquartered in Bengaluru, Center Stage Events has also handled events in Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, Mysore and other major cities pan India.

We believe in providing turnkey event management solutions by adhering to schedules and consistently extending uncompromising quality standards.

Furnishing a wholesome and authentic event experience irrespective the magnitude of the event is the business dictum of Center Stage Events.

Our Work

We curate events. We take care of the entire spectrum of activities involved in organising Corporate events. We start by building the event framework through requirement analysis followed by concise planning and client orientation. We take care of all the event activities such as on-floor operations, execution and real-time management including Stage set-up, Sound and Lighting, Theme Decoration, Tablescape, Music and DJ, Concierge Services, Logistics and Celebrity Management. And our role concludes only after post-event support!

Our expertise

Our expertise lies in our solution. We offer both standard packages and customised consultation to make each event unique for every client. We extend our capacities to the fullest to adhere to deadlines. We don't consider budget as a constraint rather a parameter to work with and offer an optimised solution. With our inhouse event architect, creative team and management and production team, we holistically organise events.

Our Approach

We believe in customer-first approach, always. We offer viable solutions which take into consideration all the factors that contribute to making an event a success. With our motto of "Bringing events to life", we strive to give each event its unique identity. With our experience and expertise we constantly strive to make the customer's event unforgettable and inspire customer loyalty; the ultimate reward for our work!

What we Do

Corporate Events

Right from employee birthday parties to culture events, we like to add that something extra to your work life! We provide ample opportunity to bring out your funky side!

HR Initiatives

With the idea to make the corporate events innovative, we arrange the entire corporate calendar for enterprises. Be it employee appreciation or their birthday celebration, even milestone celebrations and soft skill sessions, we personalise all the HR activities to match the company's values and standards.

Family Day

Introducing the family to your work is one of the most compelling activity in today's life! For Family Days, we take extra care to not just work towards bringing the diverse aspects of the company to the podium, but also to create a warm and welcoming atmosphere through exciting recreational activities. We strive to make a lasting impression of the first contact of your company with your family.

Annual Day

Bringing together the entire organisation, we ensure that all employees have the best annual extravaganza; sadly though it is celebrated just once a year! We introduce latest trends each year so that you can look forward to a different and unique Annual Day every year.

Theme Party

A superhero theme, a retro theme, or even a James Bond theme, we add merriment and tasteful party vibe to the corporate culture of hosting theme parties. We also provide multitude setting and prop options to choose from. Having successfully organised large scale theme parties, we bring to the fore our proficiency and diligence in each celebration.

Conference & Seminar

Every organisation understands the importance of enhancing the relevant skill sets of key team members in order to optimise business productivity. Keeping the company's requirement in mind, we propose and organise the choicest of conferences and seminars, with department-centric symposiums as well.

Networking Events

One should never underestimate the importance of Corporate Networking. We facilitate interactions between companies from the same as well as different industry domain, especially to initiate social interaction and networking. We bring like-minded groups together on varied dynamic platforms to create the perfect surrounding for an ambient rendezvous.

Marketing Activities

There are multiple ways to promote a brand, but there is only one way to make a lasting impact in the minds of the end-consumer. And Center Stage Events aims at precisely doing that. Creating an eventful event!

Press Conferences

Broadcasting the important company bulletins is essential not just for the company but also for the end-user. Along with arranging press conferences, we undertake all PR initiatives to facilitate quick and responsible information relay between the stakeholders.

Product Launches

We understand how important your brands and products are. And aligning with your vision, we execute classy product launches. Right from inviting the elite in the industry to presenting your product with its best foot forward, we build up the orchestra to the right pitch for the perfect product launch!

Exhibition

Once you select the exhibits to participate in, we take care of the rest. We are involved in the entire process of stall operations, branding and product presentation. While working within the set budget we ensure that the products are highlighted to the fullest and the marketing collaterals are designed to wow the audience!

Live Concerts

With the concert culture in the rising, we pride ourselves in including live concerts in our repertoire since our induction. We not just execute and manage the entire event, but also engage in pre-concert operations including the event structure and artist management. Having worked with the best recording artists in our industry like Benny Dayal and others, we try to capture the pulse of every organisation through a happening live concert!

Store Launches

And we have saved the best for last! The responsibility of opening a store is enormous. And we understand that. We organise the entire gamut of activities that are involved in a store launch; right from the decor, store theme setting and the catering; we manage the stage to the hilt. We also provide concierge service and branding and marketing guidance/orientation to bring together all the aspects uniformly.

Mall & Brand Activation

Mall brand and marketing activity management is indeed challenging! We offer premium activation services for the different brands exhibited at malls while also marketing the mall promotions itself. We recognise the competitive/aggressive brand proliferation within the market and thus provide innovative marketing concepts to up the ante ever so assertively in your favour!
